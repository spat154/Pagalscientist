## Always-on beats (run continuously alongside the calendar)

- **Film/OTT reviews** on release day — cheap, repeatable, proven.
- **Service guides** — the category's white space: visas, passports, money,
  transport, streaming, settling-in, schools. Highest long-term ROI.
- **Temple & community-infrastructure guides** — new temples, milestones.
- **Recurring news watch** — India–Australia trade/CECA, migration changes,
  cricket (Border-Gavaskar build-up to Jan 2027), RBI/rupee, the federal budget.
- **Demographics** — expand the "Indians in [city]" series with census data.
